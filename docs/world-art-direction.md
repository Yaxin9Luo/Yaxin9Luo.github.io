# Art direction and asset provenance

## Visual direction

A Gothic academy landscape with aged limestone, slate roofs, oxidized copper, mossy exposed cliffs, natural pine and ash trees, a reflective lake and a muted photographic sky. Architecture and terrain are original Three.js geometry; the rider and wraith are authored in Blender. Scanned materials and vegetation have recorded licenses. The portfolio headline is simply Yaxin Luo, with a direct introduction to multimodal design and multimodal agentic design. See the later [asset quality revision](art/asset-quality-plan.md) for the final target and reference review.

## Other assets

- 3D source: `world/src/models.js` and `world/src/world.js`; see `world-models.md`.
- Profile portrait and publication figures: original files already in the owner's website repository. No third-party scholarly figure was newly downloaded for this design.
- Cormorant Garamond and Inter: locally hosted from Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License. Original licenses are included in `world/public/fonts/`.
- Interface icon outlines and world map: original SVG markup in source.
- Ambient tones and spell audio: original Web Audio synthesis; no Harry Potter soundtrack or other copyrighted music.
- Reference: Bruno Simon's [Folio 2025](https://github.com/brunosimon/folio-2025), MIT; reviewed the README, package manifest, map and quality modules. Its frontend is inspiration, with no direct code or asset copying.
- Reference devlog: [The end? — Portfolio — Devlog 15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BE5JcpuWHG4&t=131s). Reviewed the transcript and visible portfolio footage, especially visible menus, map and teleport destinations at 1:00–4:50.
