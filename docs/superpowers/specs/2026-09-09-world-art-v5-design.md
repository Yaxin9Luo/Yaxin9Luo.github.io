# World art v5

The user authorizes autonomous implementation and visual review. The reference is `docs/art/experience-v4/landscape-refinement-target.png`; the baseline consists of `docs/evidence/experience-v4/world/` and the published v4 scene. The objective is a visibly richer, coherent magical academy and blue-green mountain landscape, not an assertion of AAA parity.

## Art decisions

- Use detailed original mountain matte art on world-fixed curved sectors, backed by a small number of deliberately shaped near ridges. Keep the dynamic sky, lake and moon. Remove repeated rounded mountain props; leave a valley behind the academy silhouette.
- Establish distinct daylight, twilight and readable moonlight. Restore material pigments, soften long tower shadows, and concentrate warm light around architecture and gathering places.
- Give paths dedicated weathered paving, dark joints and planted margins. Connect tree groups with low vegetation while leaving sightlines and walking surfaces clear. Differentiate stone, wood, copper and petals.
- Preserve full geometry, explicit quality controls, bilingual content, resume access, locomotion and optional exploration. New art remains optional phase-two loading with the existing retry/cancellation/lifetime contract.

## Evidence and acceptance

Compare actual day, dusk and night frames at highlands, court, lilac, shore and overview cameras. Inspect skyline seams and layer separation from raised and edge cameras. Night must retain visible masonry, flora colour and the rider silhouette. Roads must have legible walking centres and grounded margins; vegetation must not block entries. Capture moving views after still-image review. Preserve surface-support/navigation tests, clock continuity, loader correctness and a complete production build. Publish only after an actual rendered improvement; report performance honestly.

## Review findings

Independent environment, foreground and lighting reviewers found that the repeated mountain silhouettes, nearly equal day/night illumination, long hard shadows, wall-textured paving, bare plant margins and clone-like tree groupings are the primary problems. Additional triangle count alone will not resolve them. Technical details and iteration decisions are delegated by the user.
