# World art v5 implementation plan

**Goal:** Improve the actual academy scene through layered mountain art, intentional lighting and richer foreground materials; verify in rendered frames and publish.

**Architecture:** Existing Three.js world and optional enhancement loader. Preserve terrain heights and all interaction contracts. Art source, provenance and screenshots are versioned; generated review packages stay ignored.

### Task 1: Lighting and lake look development

Own `world/src/environment-time.js`, lighting portions of `world/src/game.js`, `createLake` in `world/src/landscape.js`, and `world/src/site-details.js`, plus directly related tests. Establish differentiated day/night values, softer horizon shadows, stronger broken water detail and less repetitive light pools. Do not change loader, navigation, content or renderer quality. Maintain finite, continuous lighting and readable night minimums. Run focused clock/atmosphere/rendering tests; controller reviews real frames before acceptance.

### Task 2: Layered landscape installation

Own `world/src/mineral-highlands.js`, backdrop integration and optional texture binding in `landscape.js`, manifest preparation for new art, and narrowly necessary game environment uniform updates. Use reviewed black-key mountain art in world-fixed curved sectors. Maintain near geometry, dynamic sky/lake and soft base transitions. Avoid repeated rounded peaks and black sky remnants. Load art via existing optional resource APIs; do not enlarge core dependencies. Verify shader compilation in actual browser and raised/edge views.

### Task 3: Foreground material and planting composition

Own ground/vegetation materials in `landscape.js`, road appearance in `world.js`, `gardens.js`, `blossom-groves.js`, `grove-foliage.js`, and relevant tests. Install dedicated CC0 paving, create controlled road margins and layered low planting, improve courtyard material separation and flower shading. Keep terrain heights, visible support triangles, steps and collision boundaries coherent. Run ground-world/surface-support/garden regression tests and review near day/night frames.

### Task 4: Integrated art review and delivery

Review all changes independently. Capture and compare reference and actual highlands/court/lilac/shore/overview in multiple lighting states. Fix concrete visual faults and capture again. Verify moving skyline and walking/flying transitions, full tests, build and resource links. Record provenance, actual evidence and performance limitations. Commit, push the feature branch, fast-forward master when safe, wait for Pages, verify live content and representative assets.
