Third-party notices for the interactive portfolio

The architecture, character clothing, guardian and broom geometry were authored
for this project. The researcher's anatomical head and hair adapt the external
CC0 assets credited below. The moon imagery is provided by NASA. These sources
are distinct from the project's generated sky and botanical artwork.

Character source geometry and hair
=================================
Blender Studio Human Base Meshes v1.4.1 — Realistic Head (Animation)
Author: Dan Ulrich / Blender Studio
License: CC0 1.0 Universal
Official source and license evidence: https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/
The retained source is an adapted head-and-eyes subset of the original bundle.

Short Messy Hair / short_messy
Author: Cortu Johnstone
License: CC0 1.0 Universal; also stated in the original material header.
Official source and license evidence:
https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/hair01.html
The original hair cards and albedo are refitted and tinted for the researcher.

Exact source downloads, retained-file hashes and adaptations are recorded in
models/characters/source/sources.json and models/characters/source/LICENSE.txt.
The runtime model inventory is models/characters/manifest.json.

Moon imagery
============
NASA Scientific Visualization Studio / Ernie Wright / NASA-GSFC / LROC
The unmodified 2K LROC color map is used on the lunar sphere.
Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720
Usage statements: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/help/
                  https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/
This imagery follows NASA's media-use guidance and the SVS public-domain
statement; it is not described as a CC0 asset. This credit identifies image
provenance and does not imply NASA endorsement.
Source URL, credit and SHA-256 are in art/night-garden/manifest.json.

Generated artwork
=================
The moonlit sky panorama and botanical atlas were generated for this project
with the built-in ImageGen tool. They are not third-party CC0 material scans.
Their generation record, actual use and hashes are in
art/night-garden/manifest.json and docs/night-garden-art.md in the repository.
