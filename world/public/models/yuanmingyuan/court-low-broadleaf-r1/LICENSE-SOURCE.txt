Original JPEG/PNG, glTF and binary files are preserved byte for byte. No texture was resized or recompressed.
Derived complete-sprays.bin partitions the original connected geometry into four canonical sprays without simplifying faces or changing UVs. The source JSON records original root translations.
The authored low crown and supporting branches are a contemporary portfolio reconstruction, not a measured historical specimen. The source is not asserted to be Buxus or an original Yuanmingyuan planting.
