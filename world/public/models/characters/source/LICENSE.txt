ACTIVE — Poly Wool Herringbone and Brown Leather
Provider: Poly Haven
Source pages: https://polyhaven.com/a/poly_wool_herringbone and https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_leather
License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal
Provider license: https://polyhaven.com/license
The retained /textures/wool-cloth and /textures/dark-leather color, OpenGL normal and roughness files supply scanned detail. The production hybrid preserves the original dyed color palette using restrained normalized scan luminance; normal and roughness maps remain non-color data. Provider scales are 0.27 m for wool and 0.40 m for leather. Authors, original provider URLs, retained-file hashes and conversions are recorded in /textures/manifest.json; the consumed hashes are repeated in sources.json.
