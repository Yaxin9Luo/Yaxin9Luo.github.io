STYLE REFERENCE: the attached picture is a reference for the PALETTE AND HAND ONLY (dull mineral azurite and malachite over warm ochre, a fine ink outline, short texture strokes, the matt faded look of an old silk handscroll). Match that palette and hand; draw the new subject described below, not the mountains in the reference. SUBJECT: a set of four separate great Heaven-Reaching Divine Fire Pillars (通天神火柱) of the immortal Yunzhongzi, each alone, well apart in a row: a tall round bronze pillar, about six times as tall as wide, with a fire-dragon coiling up it, flames licking from its seams in dull cinnabar and orange, standing on a square stone base. FRAMING: the four pillars spread across the width, each fully in the picture from base to top.
