STYLE REFERENCE: the attached picture is a reference for the PALETTE AND HAND ONLY (dull mineral azurite and malachite over warm ochre, a fine ink outline, short texture strokes, the matt faded look of an old silk handscroll). Match that palette and hand; draw the new subject described below, not the mountains in the reference. Use the azurite and malachite boldly where the subject allows (roof undersides, pines, rocks, moss, cloth), and keep a firm dark ink outline so the piece reads small. Viewpoint: from about 35 degrees above the ground, the piece facing the viewer.
SUBJECT: a long horizontal bank of night thunderclouds over a battlefield, for the Grand Tutor Wen Zhong, lord of the Ministry of Thunder (雷部): heavy coiling scrolls of outlined cloud (勾云) in deep indigo, slate blue and ink-black, their edges caught in cold silver-white light, a few forked lightning bolts drawn as thin gold-white ink lines, and half-hidden in the clouds a ring of great thunder drums (雷公鼓) and faint banners of the thunder ministry. FRAMING: the cloud bank runs from the left edge to the right edge, thickest at the top, its lower edge ragged and curling, transparent background below it.
