STYLE REFERENCE: the attached picture is a reference for the PALETTE AND HAND ONLY (dull mineral azurite and malachite over warm ochre, a fine ink outline, short texture strokes, the matt faded look of an old silk handscroll). Match that palette and hand; draw the new subject described below, not the mountains in the reference. Use the azurite and malachite boldly where the subject allows (roof undersides, pines, rocks, moss, cloth), and keep a firm dark ink outline so the piece reads small. Viewpoint: from about 35 degrees above the ground, the piece facing the viewer.
SUBJECT: a long horizontal bank of ominous storm cloud over a doomed city, in the manner of old Chinese paintings of demons: heavy coiling scrolls of outlined cloud (勾云) in ink-black, slate grey and dull purple, with a dull red demonic glow inside them and a few thin streaks of lightning; at the right end the clouds break and a few small auspicious clouds (祥云) of white and pale azurite with gold edges drift in from heaven. FRAMING: the cloud bank runs from the left edge to the right edge of the picture, thickest at the top, its lower edge ragged and curling, with transparent background below it.
