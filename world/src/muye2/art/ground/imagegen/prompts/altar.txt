SUBJECT: 祭坛, a round sacrificial altar of the Zhou army on open ground: three round tiers of rammed ochre earth, each smaller than the one below, a short stair up the front; on the top a bronze ding cauldron (鼎) on three legs, green with patina, thin grey incense smoke rising from it; around the altar a ring of eight tall wooden poles each flying a long narrow pennant, the pennants azurite blue, malachite green and ochre (no red); small stone tablets at the foot. Clearly a place of offering, recognisable at a glance.
