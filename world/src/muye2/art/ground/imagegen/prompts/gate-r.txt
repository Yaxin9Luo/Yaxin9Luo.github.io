STYLE REFERENCE: the attached picture is a reference for the PALETTE AND HAND ONLY (dull mineral azurite and malachite over warm ochre, a fine ink outline, short texture strokes, the matt faded look of an old silk handscroll). Match that palette and hand; draw the new subject described below, not the mountains in the reference. Use the azurite and malachite boldly where the subject allows (roof undersides, pines, rocks, moss, cloth), and keep a firm dark ink outline so the piece reads small. Viewpoint: from about 35 degrees above the ground, the piece facing the viewer.
SUBJECT: the gate of a mountain pass fortress of the Shang (such as 汜水关): a short thick wall of rammed yellow earth with crenellations along its top runs straight across the picture from edge to edge; in its middle a deep square gateway with heavy wooden double doors bound with bronze, shut; above the gateway a two-storey timber gate tower with a hipped thatch roof, its eaves' underside malachite green; two smaller watch towers on the wall either side; Shang standards with white and black cloth on the wall (no red). Seen from in front and a little above, the wall facing the viewer, spanning the whole width. FRAMING: the wall fills the picture from the left edge to the right edge.
