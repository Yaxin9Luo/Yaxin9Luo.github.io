A single isolated piece of distant scenery for a 2D game, painted in the manner of a Chinese blue-green landscape handscroll (青绿山水) with ruled-line architecture (界画), in the hand of a Song court painter, for a world of gods and demons (the novel Fengshen Yanyi): grander than life.
MEDIUM: ink and mineral colour on silk. A fine, even, confident ink outline round every form; mineral colours laid flat and slightly mottled: azurite blue (石青), malachite green (石绿), red-brown ochre (赭石), pale lead white, a little cinnabar. Clouds drawn as in old paintings: outlined curling bands and scrolls of cloud (勾云), not photographic. Dull, antique, matt pigments. No gradients of light, no cast shadows, no highlights, no 3D rendering.
VIEW: seen from the ground at a slight elevation, a long way off, one consistent viewpoint, front facing the viewer.
BACKGROUND: completely plain transparent background around the subject: no paper texture, no frame, no border, no text, no seal, no signature, no people.
LEGIBILITY: a bold clear silhouette that reads when shown small; large simple shapes.
AVOID: photorealism, Western painting, cartoon or anime style, Japanese temple style, curved upturned eaves and glazed tile roofs of later dynasties (this is the Shang age, about 1050 BCE: rammed-earth terraces, hipped thatch roofs with straight eaves, timber posts and galleries, bronze), modern details.
