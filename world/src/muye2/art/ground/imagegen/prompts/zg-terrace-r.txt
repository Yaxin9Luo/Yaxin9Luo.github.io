STYLE REFERENCE: the attached picture is a reference for the PALETTE AND HAND ONLY (dull mineral azurite and malachite over warm ochre, a fine ink outline, short texture strokes, the matt faded look of an old silk handscroll). Match that palette and hand; draw the new subject described below, not the mountains in the reference. Use the azurite and malachite boldly where the subject allows (roof undersides, pines, rocks, moss, cloth), and keep a firm dark ink outline so the piece reads small. Viewpoint: from about 35 degrees above the ground, the piece facing the viewer.
SUBJECT: the Deer Terrace (鹿台) of the Shang king at Zhaoge, seen from far off: a vast stepped terrace of rammed earth in three great tiers faced with white stone, a wide stair up its middle, on its summit a long splendid hall with a double hipped thatch roof and red posts, smaller halls and pavilions at its corners, many tall black and white banners on poles along its tiers. FRAMING: the terrace fills the width of the picture; the bottom edge is a straight horizontal cut across its base; nothing below it.
