SUBJECT: four separate wooden ferry boats of the Zhou army at the Mengjin crossing of the Yellow River, in a row with clear empty space between them, seen from a little above: long flat-bottomed plank boats with high square bows, each with a single tall pole and a small furled mat sail, two with rows of oars at rest, one heaped with bundled spears and shields; each on a small patch of yellow-brown water with a few wave lines that fades out at the edges. No people. Do not let them touch or overlap.
