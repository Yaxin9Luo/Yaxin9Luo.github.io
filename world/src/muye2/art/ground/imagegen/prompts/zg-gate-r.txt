STYLE REFERENCE: the attached picture is a reference for the PALETTE AND HAND ONLY (dull mineral azurite and malachite over warm ochre, a fine ink outline, short texture strokes, the matt faded look of an old silk handscroll). Match that palette and hand; draw the new subject described below, not the mountains in the reference. Use the azurite and malachite boldly where the subject allows (roof undersides, pines, rocks, moss, cloth), and keep a firm dark ink outline so the piece reads small. Viewpoint: from about 35 degrees above the ground, the piece facing the viewer.
SUBJECT: the great south gate of Zhaoge (朝歌), the Shang capital, where King Zhou comes out to fight. Grand and severe: a very high, thick gate wall of rammed yellow earth showing its ramming courses, three deep gateways with heavy bronze-studded timber doors standing open; above the middle gateway a great two-storey gate hall of timber with a double HIPPED THATCH roof with STRAIGHT eaves (no curved eaves, no tiles), red-lacquered posts, its roof underside malachite green; on each side of the gate a tall square watch-tower (阙) of rammed earth with its own thatched pavilion on top. Rows of black and white Shang banners, long streamers and bronze halberds along the wall top. A wide beaten-earth way runs out of the middle gateway toward the viewer. FRAMING: the gate and its two towers fill the picture from edge to edge; the wall is cut straight at the left and right edges so it can join a wall.
