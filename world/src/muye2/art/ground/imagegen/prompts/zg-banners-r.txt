STYLE REFERENCE: the attached picture is a reference for the PALETTE AND HAND ONLY (dull mineral azurite and malachite over warm ochre, a fine ink outline, short texture strokes, the matt faded look of an old silk handscroll). Match that palette and hand; draw the new subject described below, not the mountains in the reference. Use the azurite and malachite boldly where the subject allows (roof undersides, pines, rocks, moss, cloth), and keep a firm dark ink outline so the piece reads small. Viewpoint: from about 35 degrees above the ground, the piece facing the viewer.
SUBJECT: a set of five separate tall palace standards of the Shang court, each standing alone on its own small round base, well apart from each other in a row with clear space between them: (1) a great black banner on a tall pole with a bronze halberd top, long black streamers; (2) a white banner (白旄) of yak tails on a tall bronze-topped pole; (3) a tall pole with a bronze axe (钺) top and red-and-black streamers; (4) a black dragon banner with a long tail; (5) a pair of crossed halberds with a small canopy and hanging tassels. Each standard is tall and slender, about three times as tall as wide. Streamers blowing to the left in the wind. FRAMING: the five standards spread across the width, all the same height, each fully in the picture.
