SUBJECT: 残营, a ruined army camp after a battle: four or five tents of pale cloth on wooden frames, some still standing, some torn and collapsed; a broken palisade of sharpened stakes leaning in a broken ring; a war chariot overturned with one wheel broken; a big war drum lying on its side; two fallen standards with torn faded red-brown cloth on the ground, one standard still upright and tattered; the cold ash of camp fires ringed with stones; scattered spears. Desolate, clearly a battlefield camp, spread wide and low.
FRAMING: the place fills the picture almost from edge to edge (only a small margin), centred, its own patch of ground spreading under it and fading at the edges.
