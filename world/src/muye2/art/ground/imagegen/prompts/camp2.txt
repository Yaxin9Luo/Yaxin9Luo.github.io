SUBJECT: 残营, an abandoned army camp: four or five tents of pale cloth on wooden frames, two still standing and the others slack and fallen in; a palisade of sharpened stakes, broken in places, in a loose ring; an old chariot tipped on its side with one wheel off; a large drum lying on its side; two faded red-brown standards fallen on the ground and one still upright and frayed; cold hearths ringed with stones; scattered spears and baskets. Quiet and deserted, spread wide and low.
FRAMING: the camp fills the picture almost from edge to edge (only a small margin), centred, its own patch of trodden earth spreading under it and fading at the edges.
