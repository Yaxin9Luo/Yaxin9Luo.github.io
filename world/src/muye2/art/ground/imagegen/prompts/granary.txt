SUBJECT: 粮仓, a granary yard of the Zhou: three round granaries (囷) of woven wattle plastered with ochre clay, each on a low stone footing, each with a tall conical thatch roof with a knob at its peak and a small dark hatch; beside them a long low barn with a hipped thatch roof; a wattle fence round part of the yard; heaps of golden millet and a few tied grain sacks on the beaten earth; an empty ox-cart with two big spoked wheels. Rustic and plentiful.
