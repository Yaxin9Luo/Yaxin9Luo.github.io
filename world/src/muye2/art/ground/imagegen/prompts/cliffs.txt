SUBJECT: four separate tall cliff faces for the sides of a narrow mountain pass, standing in a row with clear empty space between them, each a steep wall of rock about twice as tall as it is wide: angular blue-green rock built with axe-cut texture strokes (斧劈皴), azurite on the upper faces and malachite on the ledges over warm ochre, a few small pines clinging to ledges, moss dots, scree at the foot. Do not let them touch or overlap.
