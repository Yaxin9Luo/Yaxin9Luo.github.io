SUBJECT: 封神台, the Platform of Investiture raised on Mount Qi (岐山) in the novel Fengshen Yanyi. A tall, grand terrace of rammed yellow earth in three square stages, each smaller than the one below, each edged by a low wooden railing; a long straight stone stair climbs the middle of its front face from the ground to the top. On the top stage a timber hall with a double hipped thatch roof (四阿重屋), the eaves' underside painted malachite green; in its open door hangs a long pale scroll (the List, 封神榜). Four tall poles fly long hanging apricot-yellow banners (杏黄旗) at the corners of the top stage. Wisps of white cloud curl round the foot of the terrace. Two old pines with malachite needle masses stand at its foot, one each side. Solemn, the grandest place on the field.
