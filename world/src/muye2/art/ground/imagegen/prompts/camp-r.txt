STYLE REFERENCE: the attached picture is a reference for the PALETTE AND HAND ONLY (dull mineral azurite and malachite over warm ochre, a fine ink outline, short texture strokes, the matt faded look of an old silk handscroll). Match that palette and hand; draw the new subject described below, not the mountains in the reference. Viewpoint: from about 35 degrees above the ground, the piece facing the viewer.
SUBJECT: 残营, a ruined army camp after a battle: four or five tents of pale cloth on wooden frames, some still standing, some torn and collapsed; a broken palisade of sharpened stakes leaning in a broken ring; a war chariot overturned with one wheel broken; a big war drum lying on its side; two fallen standards with torn faded red-brown cloth on the ground, one standard still upright and tattered; the cold ash of camp fires ringed with stones; scattered spears.
