SUBJECT: a long straight stretch of the city wall of Zhaoge (朝歌), the Shang capital: a very high wall of rammed yellow earth showing its ramming courses, crenellations along the top, a corner tower with a hipped thatch roof at the right end, black and white Shang standards and a few bronze-topped poles on the wall; at its foot a dry moat edge and some scattered rubble. Seen from in front and a little above. FRAMING: the wall runs from the left edge of the picture to the right edge.
